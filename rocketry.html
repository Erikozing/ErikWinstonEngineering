Rose Propulsion Laboratory

Going into my fourth year of involvement with the Rose Propulsion Laboratory, I'm incredibly proud of what we've accomplished. Starting from nothing in 2023 all the way to hotfire testing our first liquid engine has taught me what the real struggles of a large‑scope engineering problem are and how to deal with them. While learning in the classroom has given me technical proficiency, it's this experience that makes me a valuable member of an engineering team.

While at Rose Propulsion Laboratory, I have served as:

  • Controls Team Lead Responsible for the control algorithm, instrumentation, electrical components, power distribution, and communications.
  • Test Director Officiated hotfire, coldflow, hydrostatic, and igniter testing for the Liquid Engine 1 test campaign.
  • Operations Lead Implemented administrative restructuring and task tracking systems; prepared onboarding presentations and resources for new members.

This year, I plan to work primarily on GNC development, guiding the team down a path of controller co‑design to ensure we create models and control strategies compatible with our physical system.
