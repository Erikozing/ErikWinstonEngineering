Projects

Liquid Propelled Rocket Lander Control Algorithm

To support GNC programs at Rose Propulsion Laboratory and enable controller co-design, I'm developing control algorithms to evaluate combinations of different linearization techniques and control strategies in application for our liquid propelled rocket lander. Controls strategies under test include model predictive control, lookup table control, and more exciting (for me), Machine Learning Control. Linearization techniques under test include standard Jacobian, numerical, and gain scheduling.

University Student Launch Initiative

While working with Rose Rocketry's USLI team, I helped develop the RF payload, tasked with gathering pressure, temperature, altitude, and other flight data from 8,000 ft, then transmitting it back to a ground station using an STM32 microcontroller and a HackRF radio module. RF engineering concepts were completely foreign to me at first, but I welcomed the opportunity to broaden my engineering skills.

Milwaukee Tool Pipe Camera Skid

I worked with a team of my peers to develop a camera skid to improve the pipe inspection capability of the Milwaukee Tool Pipe Camera. After getting customer feedback from plumbers, we produced a design that uses two attachments with compliant arms and added lighting elements to provide better visibility and corner‑rounding capability to the tool, then communicated our final product to Milwaukee Tool in a formal presentation. This project served as my introduction to structured engineering process models and greatly informed how I have approached subsequent project management.

Variable‑Shape Sailing Keel

In accordance with my fascination with sailing, I conducted a year‑long research project into a novel keel design that uses a two‑part keel with a hinge in the middle to allow it to change shape. By hinging the keel, it could form an airfoil shape, directing lift to either port or starboard—allowing the keel to better resist the moment exerted by the wind on the sail, giving the boat a higher top speed. After fabricating a proof‑of‑concept model and testing in an endless pool, I produced an IEEE paper to communicate the findings.
